## Visible company identity
The official full-color horizontal Carpenter Technology symbol and wordmark is shown above the fold on a white field, paired with “Candidate vision ... by Russell Dudek.” It is used only for nominative identification and is not altered, recolored, cropped, animated, merged, or presented as official authorship.

## Logo-use decision and rationale
The official brand guide identifies the full-color horizontal symbol and wordmark as the preferred version. The campaign respects the 144-pixel digital / 1.5-inch print minimum, clear space, light-field guidance, and non-modification rules.

## Typography evidence
The official guide specifies DIN Next LT Pro for print, URW DIN for web, and Calibri as the alternate when DIN is unavailable.

## Typography decision
No proprietary font files are copied. The implementation uses Calibri / Trebuchet MS for body text and Arial Narrow / Calibri for condensed all-cap display behavior, reproducing the brand's engineered, condensed hierarchy without claiming those substitutes are official DIN fonts.

## Photography / illustration character
The campaign does not use generic stock photography. Its primary original visual is a role-derived crystalline reliability lattice, reflecting Carpenter's specialty-materials context without copying a proprietary product diagram or implying knowledge of internal processes.

## Geometry and interface motifs
Precise condensed headlines, light fields for official identity, Carpenter red decision emphasis, CT black technical surfaces, cyan signal paths, iron/titanium neutrals, fine engineering grids, and angular hexagonal lattice nodes.

## Motion character
A stress/signal pulse traverses the complete lattice while scenario selection changes weak and active bonds. Motion explains how incomplete connections degrade reliability decision quality. Reduced-motion mode removes travel while retaining every state and label.

## Recommended use by artifact
- Website: full brand system plus Reliability Lattice.
- Resume and cover letter: restrained red rules, condensed headings, official role/company line, and white document field.
- Brief, entry plan, and product review: same tokens, angular technical callouts, and decision-oriented tables.

## Independent-candidate distinction
Every public surface identifies the work as an independent candidate vision by Russell Dudek and includes a non-endorsement statement.
