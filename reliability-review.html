1 · Qualify the decision, not the technology

DimensionReview questionEvidence to collect
ConsequenceWhat asset, safety, quality, delivery, or economic outcome is exposed?Criticality, downtime category, customer/process consequence
Recurrence / burdenIs this repeated enough, costly enough, or risky enough to learn from?Event history, labor/rework, recurring machine or quality issues
ActionabilityWhat decision or work step can change when insight arrives?Decision owner, threshold, response window, work route
Data readinessCan asset, work, condition, failure, and material context be connected?Completeness, lineage, timing, naming, ownership
Adoption burdenWhat must planners, technicians, operators, engineers, or buyers do differently?Workflow steps, cognitive load, training, incentives, exceptions
ReuseIs the capability portable across sites or asset classes?Common object model, legitimate variation, integration pattern

2 · Define the minimum reliability data contract

Required context

  • Asset identity, hierarchy, and criticality
  • Observed condition and timestamp
  • Work order, task, labor, and closure evidence
  • Failure mode, mechanism, cause, and uncertainty
  • Material consumed, substitute, lead time, and availability
  • Decision, owner, date, and expected effect

Governance that changes work

  • Named owner for each reusable data product
  • Quality checks embedded in workflow
  • Controlled vocabulary with exception path
  • Discoverable lineage and consumption
  • Feedback when data is insufficient
  • Retirement rules for unused data capture

3 · Bound AI by work and authority

AI roleAppropriate whenHuman authority
DetectSignal and context are sufficient to surface a meaningful anomaly.Confirm relevance; reject noise.
PrioritizeConsequence, urgency, capacity, and dependencies can be compared.Approve queue and tradeoffs.
RecommendOptions, rationale, uncertainty, and evidence are visible.Select, modify, defer, or escalate.
ExplainPattern and source evidence can be traced.Validate against domain knowledge.
AutomateBounded low-risk action, tested controls, fallback, owner, and monitoring exist.Set policy, monitor exceptions, halt.

4 · Make the product decision

Scale

Observed value, usable workflow, governed evidence, clear ownership, and a reusable architecture pattern.

Adapt

Core capability transfers, but asset, site, or process variation is legitimate and explicitly bounded.

Hold

Consequence may be real, but actionability, data, ownership, or adoption is not ready.

Buy / configure

The capability is commodity and fits enterprise architecture without surrendering the decision model.

Build

The workflow, integration, data product, or decision experience is genuinely differentiating.

Stop

The use case cannot produce enough value or learning to justify its operating burden.

5 · Close the learning loop

Record what changed in the work, what evidence improved, what users accepted or overrode, what outcome was observed, what should be standardized, and what should remain local. A pilot that leaves no reusable asset is only a demonstration.

Assumption discipline: No score or percentage is assigned without a Carpenter baseline. Each field is a review question, not a judgment about current maturity.
