Interview thesis brief

Reliability should learn under load.

Nominal role: Sr. Manager - Digital Product & Reliability.

Actual mandate: Convert maintenance and reliability from a fragmented set of local practices, systems, and data into a reusable enterprise product system that improves uptime, maintenance effectiveness, spare-parts economics, and decision quality.

Thesis: Reliability scales when every maintenance event strengthens the next decision. Asset identity, work execution, condition, failure evidence, materials, and strategy must function as one learning lattice.

The company moment

The role description itself signals a transition: Carpenter wants improved availability and standardized work management now, while building governed data products and a scalable foundation for predictive, prescriptive, and autonomous reliability. Recent public reporting also points to strong demand and capacity economics, increasing the value of dependable uptime. This is public context, not internal performance evidence.

Executive interview questions

  1. Which availability losses have enterprise leverage rather than only local leverage?

  3. What is the intended balance between SAP PM standardization and surrounding digital products?
  4. Which site or asset class is the best proving ground for a closed-loop reliability product?
